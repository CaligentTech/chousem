ABOUT US

Our Story

C HOUSE is an Italian Bistro, Bar & Lounge in Jebel Ali, Dubai, bringing together contemporary Italian favourites, an Asian Kitchen, crafted cocktails and relaxed social dining. From casual lunches and aperitivo to lively evenings, live entertainment and outdoor dining, C HOUSE is designed as a neighbourhood destination for good food, great drinks and memorable times.

What makes us special

From exceptional Italian dining and premium shisha to live unplugged Bollywood beats, C House brings Dubai's complete day-to-night vibe together.

Indulge in authentic Italian cuisine rolled fresh daily by our culinary team. From hand-crafted truffle pastas and wood-fired pizzas to delicate antipasti and artisanal desserts, each dish is designed to be a memorable ritual.

Explore an inspired beverage menu featuring bespoke artisan mocktails, fine Italian wines, curated aperitivo selections, and refreshing signature concoctions tailored to elevate your dining journey.

Immerse yourself in our electrifying entertainment lineup—from soulful acoustic sessions and jazz Fridays to our legendary weekend unplugged Bollywood nights that keep the energy alive.

Host unforgettable birthdays, corporate gatherings, and intimate milestones with dedicated event planning, personalized menus, and our breathtaking indoor and terrace spaces.
